# A clearer starting point for your next client contract

If you freelance or consult, a client agreement can contain details you want to understand before you decide what to do next: payment timing, revision limits, confidentiality, or what happens when a project ends.

I built [LegalClarify](https://legalclarify.app/?utm_source=ronmashate_blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=stripe_launch_20261021) to help you work through that text. It produces an AI-assisted summary, key points and discussion prompts to compare with your original agreement.

## Start with a fictional example

You don't have to upload a real contract to explore the workflow. [Open the fictional demo](https://legalclarify.app/demo?utm_source=ronmashate_blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=stripe_launch_20261021) and see the kind of details worth checking.

For your own analysis, use pasted text, a TXT file or a PDF with selectable text. For Word files or scans, prepare a readable copy and check it against the original.

AI can miss context or make mistakes. LegalClarify is an educational tool, not a law firm or a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer.
